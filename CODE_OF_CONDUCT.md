# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Enclavier to be a project where anyone can take part without harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient with other people,
- respecting different opinions and experience,
- giving and accepting constructive feedback,
- owning mistakes and learning from them,
- focusing on what is best for the people who rely on the project.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention,
- insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling,
- public or private harassment,
- publishing someone's private information without their permission,
- anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break them, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **team@codearrive.in**. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
